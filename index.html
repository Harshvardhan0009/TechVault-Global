02 — History

Technology Milestones

Key inventions that reshaped civilisation, decade by decade.

1876

Telephone Invented

Alexander Graham Bell patents the telephone, connecting human voices across distance for the first time and founding the modern telecommunications industry.

First Powered Flight

The Wright brothers achieve 12 seconds of controlled, powered flight at Kitty Hawk — launching the age of aviation and transforming global transport forever.

1903
1947

Transistor Created

Bell Labs engineers invent the transistor — the foundation of every digital device. Its miniaturisation would eventually put billions of processors in human hands.

ARPANET Goes Live

The precursor to the internet transmits its first message between UCLA and Stanford, planting the seed of a global network that would redefine communication, commerce, and culture.

1969
1990

World Wide Web

Tim Berners-Lee proposes the World Wide Web at CERN, turning the internet from an academic network into a global public information space.

Smartphone Era Begins

Apple launches the iPhone, converging phone, camera, and computer into a pocket-sized device and triggering the mobile revolution that now shapes daily life worldwide.

2007
2022

Generative AI Breakthrough

Large language models go mainstream, marking a new era in which machines can generate text, code, art, and scientific hypotheses — redefining the boundary of human–machine collaboration.
